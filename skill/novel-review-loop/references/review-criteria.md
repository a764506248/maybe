# Review Criteria

Use these criteria when the task requires scoring or a formal review record. Scores are evidence summaries, not targets for prose optimization.

## Weighted criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What to inspect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Continuity | 10 | Time, location, knowledge, emotion, cause, and unresolved prior events. |
| Internal plausibility | 12 | Character behavior, institutions, technology, and social responses under established conditions. |
| Plot movement | 12 | Changed circumstances, relationships, knowledge, choices, risk, or irreversible cost. |
| Pace and redundancy | 10 | Repeated scenes, images, conclusions, or paragraphs without a new function. |
| Reader pull | 10 | Concrete dilemmas and consequences that create a forward question. |
| Thoughtful dark tone | 14 | Harm emerging from defensible choices, systems, incentives, or competing goods. |
| Character agency | 7 | A choice that affects consequences, including a meaningful choice to comply. |
| Motivation and cost | 7 | Desire, fear, practical interest, and a visible price after action. |
| Conflict escalation | 5 | A later conflict changes the nature of the earlier one rather than only increasing volume. |
| Information control | 4 | Foreshadowing and reversals alter understanding without arbitrary concealment. |
| World manifestation | 3 | Prices, procedures, space, work, relationships, and ordinary choices carry the worldbuilding. |
| Thematic restraint | 3 | Events permit inference without repeated narration of their meaning. |
| Setup and payoff | 1 | Important details develop, resolve, or are deliberately retired. |
| Emotional specificity | 2 | A particular person loses a habit, relationship, identity, or choice—not merely “society worsens.” |

Interpretation: 85–100 pass; 75–84 conditional pass; 60–74 revise; below 60 rebuild the chapter function before line editing.

## Hard gates

Do not pass a chapter when any of these remains:

1. A core character makes an unexplained choice solely to move the plot.
2. The chapter repeats the previous chapter's function without a new irreversible consequence.
3. A key turn works only because the framework explains missing prose.
4. Darkness is produced mainly by gloomy language, spectacle, or narrator argument.
5. Confirmed canon is materially contradicted without an explanation in the text.

## Formal review record

When the project uses a review log, include:

1. One-sentence judgment of what the chapter actually accomplishes.
2. Criterion scores with one piece of prose evidence each.
3. Three reader questions.
4. At most three issues ordered by impact.
5. A minimal revision plan.
6. A dark-tone check identifying whether the effect comes from choice/system or only atmosphere.
7. A conclusion: pass, conditional pass, revise, or fail.

For a post-edit re-review, retain the pre-edit score and append the new score plus the precise reason for any change. For a five-chapter boundary, also check protagonist cadence, repeated chapter functions, dormant setups, ending devices, and whether macro change has reached a concrete life.
