# Project invariants

Read this reference for the novel in the `xiaosho` workspace.

## Core meaning

- AI is neither the final antagonist nor a central ruler. It breaks the old balance between labor, wages, consumption, and production.
- Financial collapse, social reorganization, the Taiwan operation, the China–Japan war, and Japan's end result from human choices under fear, interest, pride, belonging, revenge, and displaced responsibility.
- The story is a thought experiment with gradual cognitive reversals. What appears progressive, safe, fair, or necessary should reveal costs through experience rather than authorial declaration.

## Causal spine

Preserve the sequence and allow stages to overlap:

```text
AI and automation raise efficiency
→ employment contracts
→ consumption weakens
→ services and smaller firms fail
→ industry, property, and public revenue weaken
→ household and business debt defaults spread
→ banks and public services fail unevenly
→ temporary relief becomes long-term basic security
→ hierarchy and the search for meaning return
→ collective victory fills the vacuum
→ Taiwan's political and military system is overcome within one week
→ the East Asian security dilemma shifts toward Japan
→ China–Japan war
→ Japan's end
```

Do not jump from layoffs directly to financial collapse, war, or the new welfare settlement without dramatizing the intermediate mechanisms.

## Protagonist functions

### Programmer

His broad movement is from benefiting from AI, tolerating others' removal, being removed himself, surviving through a new settlement, and discovering that survival does not resolve dignity, hierarchy, or his need for collective meaning.

He is not a world-saving genius. His strength is understanding part of the mechanism while remaining implicated in it.

### Special-forces operator

His broad movement is from proving himself through obedience and protection, learning that order differs from justice, gaining purpose and compromised heroism in the Taiwan operation, and later recognizing that war both terrifies him and makes him necessary.

He is not a brute or a simple mouthpiece for state power. Give him operationally reasonable choices whose human costs remain real.

## Setting boundaries

- There is no omnipotent central AI that independently governs cities, medicine, supplies, or war.
- Automation is uneven, dependent on hardware, energy, maintenance, remote takeover, and human organization.
- Basic security eventually becomes unconditional and coexists with money, markets, private firms, unequal income, and social status.
- Taiwan's “one week” means the old central political and military system loses organized resistance; it does not mean every conflict or integration problem ends in one week.
- The China–Japan war must emerge through escalating security dilemmas with multiple missed stopping opportunities, not from one mad leader, one AI decision, or one isolated accident.
